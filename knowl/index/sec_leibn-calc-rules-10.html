As a result the rules governing these differentials are very modern in appearance:
\begin{align*} \dx{(\text{ constant } )}\amp =0\\ \dx{(z-y+w+x)}\amp =\dx{ z}-\dx{ y}+\dx{ w}+\dx{ x}\\ \dx{(xv)}\amp =x\dx{ v}+v\dx{ x}\\ \dx{\left(\frac{v}{y}\right)}\amp =\frac{y\dx{ v}-v\dx{ y}}{yy} \end{align*}
and, when \(a\) is an integer:
\begin{align*} \dx{(x^a)}\amp =ax^{a-1}\dx{ x}\text{.} \end{align*}
